Biography

Elon Musk was born on June 28, 1971, in Pretoria, South Africa. He is widely regarded as one of the most influential entrepreneurs and visionaries of the 21st century.

From a young age, Musk showed exceptional talent in technology. At just 12 years old, he created and sold his first video game called "Blastar" for $500.

After moving to Canada and later the United States, he earned dual bachelor's degrees in Physics and Economics from the University of Pennsylvania. He briefly attended Stanford University for a PhD in energy physics but dropped out after two days to pursue his entrepreneurial dreams.

His first major success came with Zip2, an online city guide software, which was sold for nearly $300 million in 1999. He then co-founded X.com, which later became PayPal. In 2002, PayPal was sold to eBay for $1.5 billion, giving Musk the capital to pursue his bigger ambitions.

With this wealth, he founded SpaceX in 2002 with the goal of reducing space transportation costs and making humanity a multi-planetary species. In 2004, he joined Tesla Motors (now Tesla, Inc.) and became its CEO, leading the company to revolutionize the electric vehicle industry.

Today, Elon Musk leads multiple groundbreaking companies including Tesla, SpaceX, Neuralink, The Boring Company, and xAI. He also owns X (formerly Twitter), which he acquired in 2022.

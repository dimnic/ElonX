Key Achievements

2002 — Founded SpaceX

Goal: Make space travel affordable and enable human settlement on Mars.

2008 — First successful Falcon 1 launch

First privately developed liquid-fueled rocket to reach orbit.

2012 — Dragon spacecraft docks with ISS

First commercial spacecraft to deliver cargo to the International Space Station.

2018 — Falcon Heavy launch

Most powerful operational rocket at the time. Sent Tesla Roadster into space.

2020 — First crewed mission by Crew Dragon

First private company to send astronauts to the ISS.

2022 — Acquired Twitter (now X)

Rebranded and transformed the platform with focus on free speech.

2023 — Founded xAI

Company dedicated to understanding the true nature of the universe.
